Shopify App

Cart Insights

Cart Insights helps Shopify merchants monitor live cart activity, view customer navigation timelines, track storefront cart behavior before checkout, and view summary report data for analytics and marketing.

  • See customer activity, navigation, and cart contents in real time
  • Understand each customer's journey from cart to order
  • Collect traffic data including landing page, referrer, geolocation, and device context
  • Export cart activity and summary reports to CSV for analysis and marketing
